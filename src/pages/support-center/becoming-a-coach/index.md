## Coach Preview

Coach functionality is currently in a limited preview while we work with a small number of coaches. Selecting **I'm a coach** during onboarding, or switching to a coach account in **Account Settings**, asks for a **Coach Preview access code**.

The code is issued free of charge to invited coaches. It is not sold, and can't be purchased anywhere. It exists only to limit how many coaches join during the preview period.

## Request a code

We're looking for feedback from Australian running coaches. If you coach runners and want to try Chiron with your athletes, [get in touch](/contact/#coaches) and tell us a little about how you coach: roughly how many athletes, what they train for, and what you use to plan their training now.

## Switch to a coach account during onboarding

1. On the first onboarding screen, choose **Coach**.
2. Enter your **Coach Preview access code**.
3. Continue through the rest of onboarding. When you finish, you'll land on **My Athletes**.

## Switch an existing account to coach

1. Go to **More › Settings › Account Settings**.
2. Under account type, choose **Coach**. You'll see *Coach Preview access code required*.
3. Enter your code and tap **Save**.

You only need to enter a code once. If you switch back to Athlete later, your athletes, invitations and coaching data are kept, and you can switch back to Coach without a new code.

## Coaches still have their own training

As a coach you keep your own schedule. Use **My Training** in the athlete picker to switch back to it. See [Viewing and Planning an Athlete's Training](/support-center/planning-athlete-training/).
