A club is home for your race events and race series. **To create a race event or a race series, you must first create a club.**

Currently users must have a coach account to create a club.

## Create a club

1. Go to **More → Clubs**.
2. Tap **Create Club**.
3. Enter a **Club Name**. Description, Website and Public contact are optional.
4. Tap **Create Club** to save.

You become the club's first administrator.

The **Public contact** is an email address or a web link. Entrants see it as a "Contact organiser" link on your event registration pages.

## Edit a club

1. Go to **More → Clubs** and open the club.
2. Tap **Edit**, update the details, and tap **Update Club**.

To delete a club, open its menu and choose **Delete**. Deleting a club also deletes its events, start lists and results, and can't be undone.

## Add a club administrator

Administrators can manage everything in the club: its events, start lists, teams, race timing, results and series. Only the club creator needs a coach account. **Any user can be added as an administrator.**

1. Open the club and find **Administrators**.
2. Tap **Add Administrator**.
3. Enter the person's account email address. To add several people at once, enter up to 10 email addresses, one per line.
4. Tap **Add**.

**The user's account email address is required to add them.** It must belong to an existing Chiron account — Chiron doesn't send an invitation to someone who doesn't have one yet. Ask them to sign up first, then add them.

Once added, the administrator sees **Clubs** in their More menu. They can manage the club but can't create a new club unless they have a coach account.

### Remove an administrator

Open the administrator's menu in the Administrators list and choose **Remove Administrator**. A club always keeps at least one administrator, so you can't remove the last one.
