A race series groups several of your club's events — a summer series, a cross-country league, a parkrun-style season — so entrants are followed, and ranked, across every round. Series belong to a club, so [create a club](/support-center/create-and-manage-a-club) first.

## Create a series

1. Go to **More → Clubs**, open your club, and tap **Series**.
2. Tap **New Series**.
3. Enter a **Series Title**. Sport Type, Status, Description, Race Type, Registration URL and the **Include sex / age category / team** options are optional.
4. Save the series.

To change a series later, open it and tap **Edit**. Deleting a series removes its participants and standings; its events stay in your club.

## Add events to a series

There are two ways:

- **From an event** — open the event, tap **Edit**, turn on **Part of a series**, and choose the series. You can also create a new series from here.
- **From the series** — open the series, tap **Events**, then **New Event**. The new event is already attached to the series.

An event can belong to one series, and only to a series of the same club. When you add an event that already has entrants, they're linked to the series straight away.

## Remove an event from a series

Open the event, tap **Edit**, and remove it from its series in the **Series** section, then confirm. The event and its results stay in your club; they just stop counting towards the series.

## Series participants

Open the series and tap **Participants** to see everyone who has entered any of its events. Chiron recognises the same person across events when their first and last name match and their email or year of birth matches too, so it helps to record email or year of birth for every entrant.

## Scoring and standings

1. Open the series and tap **Series Scoring**.
2. Tap **Enable Scoring**, then **Save Settings**.

How points work:

- In each event, an entrant scores points equal to their finishing position **within their sex category** — 1 point for first, 2 for second, and so on.
- Anyone who didn't finish, didn't start, or didn't enter that event scores one point more than the slowest finisher in their category.
- Points are added up across the series, and **the lowest total wins**. Standings are ranked separately for each sex category.

Only events marked **Final Results** count. Standings update automatically whenever an event in the series is marked final. To update them by hand, tap **Force Recalculate Series Standings** on the scoring screen.

Open **Series Standings** to see the table, and tap a participant to see their result in each event.
