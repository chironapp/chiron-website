Notes sit on your schedule next to your workouts and events. Use them for anything that isn't a workout: how you slept, a niggle you're watching, travel days, a change to your plan, or information for your coach.

## Create a note

1. Open the **Schedule** tab.
2. Tap **+**, then **Note**. You can also tap a day and choose **Note** from **Add to this day**.
3. Give the note a **Title** and a **Date**.
4. Write your note in **Content**.
5. Tap **Save**.

## Edit or duplicate a note

Open the note and tap the **⋮** menu:

- **Edit** — change the title, date or content.
- **Duplicate** — copy the note to a new date.

## Notes and your coach

If you have a coach, they can see your notes and comment on them, and you can reply. A note with comments shows a comment indicator on your schedule. See [Comments on Workouts and Notes](/support-center/workout-and-note-comments/).
