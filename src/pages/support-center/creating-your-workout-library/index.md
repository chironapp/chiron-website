## What is the Workout Library?

The Workout Library is a collection of saved workouts you can schedule to any date on your calendar — or to your athletes' calendars if you're a coach. Any workout type can be saved to the library: run sessions, strength, cross training, and more.

## Organise with Categories

Categories are folders for your Workout Library — group workouts by type, training phase, or whatever makes sense for how you coach or train.

1. In the Workout Library, tap **Add → Category**.
2. Give it a name (e.g. _Track Sessions_, _Race Week_, _Rehab_).
3. To add a workout to a category, open the category and add a new workout, or set the category in the new/edit workout screen.

## Create a new workout in the Library

1. Navigate to the Workout Library: **Schedule → Add → Library Workout**, or **More → Workout Library**.
2. Tap **Add → Workout** and set the activity type and category.
3. Fill in the workout details, then tap **Save**.

For strength and physical therapy workouts, see [Strength & Conditioning in Chiron](/support-center/strength-and-conditioning) for a full walkthrough of adding and managing exercises.

## Save a scheduled workout to the Library

If you build a one-off workout directly on the Schedule and want to reuse it later:

1. Tap the workout on the Schedule to open it.
2. Tap the three-dot menu → **Add to Workout Library**.

The workout is added to your Library and can be scheduled to any date from there.

## Duplicate a Library workout

Duplicating lets you create a variation — a progressive overload version, for example — without modifying the original.

1. In the Workout Library, find the workout.
2. Tap the three-dot menu → **Duplicate**.
3. A copy appears in the Library — tap it to rename and edit.
