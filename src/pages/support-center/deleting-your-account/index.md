You can delete your Chiron account at any time from the app. **Deletion is permanent and can't be undone.**

## Delete your account

1. Go to **More › Settings › Account Settings**.
2. Tap **Delete Account**.
3. Type **delete** to confirm, then confirm the deletion.

## What's deleted

- Your profile and sign-in account
- Your workouts, events, notes, comments and other training data
- Your coaching relationships. Your coach, or your athletes, lose access
- Chiron's connection to Strava. Chiron revokes its access to your Strava account. Your activities on Strava itself are not affected

## What's kept

If you've taken part in club races timed with Chiron, those race results stay in the club's published results, but they're no longer linked to your account.

## Coaches

If you're a coach, deleting your account ends your coaching relationships. Your athletes' accounts and training stay with them.

## Before you delete

If you just want to stop syncing from Strava, you can [disconnect Strava](/support-center/syncing-with-strava/) instead. If something isn't working, [let us know](/support-center/contact/). We may be able to help.

For how we handle your data, see our [Privacy Policy](/privacy/).
