## Get the app

Chiron is available for iPhone on the [App Store](/download/) and in your browser at [beta.chironapp.com](https://beta.chironapp.com/). Your account works on both, so anything you plan on your phone shows up on the web and vice versa. Android is coming soon; until then Android users can use the web app.

## Create your account

1. Open Chiron and tap **Don't have an account? Register here**.
2. Enter your email address and a password.
3. Check your inbox and confirm your email address using the link we send you.

## Choose Athlete or Coach

The first onboarding screen asks how you'll use Chiron:

- **Athlete** — plan and track your own training. Most people start here.
- **Coach** — manage training for athletes you coach. Coach features are currently in a limited preview and need a Coach Preview access code. See [Becoming a Coach in Chiron](/support-center/becoming-a-coach/).

You can change this later in **Settings › Account Settings**.

## Finish setting up

Onboarding then walks you through a few quick steps:

1. **About you** — your name and a few profile details.
2. **Profile photo** — optional, but it helps your coach or athletes recognise you.
3. **Connect Strava** — Chiron automatically imports new activities and up to four years of training history on first sync. You can skip this and connect later. See [Syncing Your Training with Strava](/support-center/syncing-with-strava/). <!-- CONFIRM: initial import window is 4 years (STRAVA_INITIAL_IMPORT_DAYS=1460); update if reduced to 1-2 years for launch -->
4. **Next event** — optionally add the race you're training for, with its name and date.

If a coach has already invited you, you'll also be offered their invitation during onboarding. Accepting is optional. See [Working with a Coach](/support-center/working-with-a-coach/).

## Finding your way around

Chiron has four main tabs:

- **Home** — your next workout and next event at a glance.
- **Schedule** — your training calendar. Tap **+** to add a workout, event, note, or import results and activities.
- **Insights** — your personal bests and longer-term training summaries.
- **More** — Settings, your Workout Library, My Performance Team (your coaches), Help and Feedback.
