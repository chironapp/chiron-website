**Hide future training** lets you plan a training block well ahead without your athletes seeing all of it. Plans change, and a long list of sessions can overwhelm an athlete or tempt them to train ahead. With the setting on, your athletes only see the training you've planned up to a set number of weeks ahead. The rest stays hidden until it gets closer.

You still see everything on your athletes' schedules.

## Turn on Hide future training

1. Go to **More › Settings**.
2. Under **Coach › Athlete schedule**, turn on **Hide future training**.
3. In **Visible to athletes**, choose how far ahead your athletes can see: 1 to 6 weeks.

The hint below the setting tells you the last day your athletes can currently see, for example "Athletes can see training you've planned up to Sunday 13 December 2026". It updates as soon as you change the setting.

The setting applies to all your athletes. You can't set a different period for each athlete.

## How far ahead athletes can see

Chiron's weeks run Monday to Sunday. With **1 week**, your athletes see the rest of this week and all of next week, up to Sunday. Each extra week adds another seven days.

The line moves forward a whole week at the start of each Monday, so a new week of training appears for your athletes every Monday without you doing anything. Weeks are worked out in UTC. In Australia that means Monday morning, and in the Americas it's Sunday evening.

Today's training is never hidden, whatever period you choose.

## What gets hidden

A workout or note is hidden from your athlete when:

- you created it on their schedule,
- you have **Hide future training** on, and
- its date is after the last day your athletes can see.

Whether it's completed makes no difference. Events and labels are never hidden, so your athlete can always see their upcoming races and the periods you've marked on their schedule.

Workouts and notes your athlete created themselves are never hidden. If your athlete has more than one coach, your setting only applies to the workouts and notes you created.

Workouts and notes created before Hide future training was available count as your athlete's own, so they stay visible. To hide one of these, duplicate it and delete the original. ## How hidden training looks to you

On an athlete's schedule, a hidden workout or note shows a crossed-out eye at the top right of its card, and the card is slightly faded. You see this in schedule search results too. Every coach of the athlete sees the same thing, including for items another coach created.

You can edit, move and delete hidden workouts and notes as normal.

## What your athlete sees

For your athlete, hidden workouts and notes don't exist yet. They don't appear on the schedule or in search, and your athlete can't open, edit or delete them. When the line moves past them, they appear on the schedule.

If your athlete tries to move one of your workouts or notes past the line, Chiron stops them and shows: "Your coach hasn't released training that far ahead. Choose an earlier date."

## Comments on hidden training

You can comment on a hidden workout or note. Your athlete isn't notified while it's hidden. When it becomes visible, your comments show as unread for them. See [Comments on Workouts and Notes](/support-center/workout-and-note-comments/).

## Turn it off, or stop coaching an athlete

- **Turn the setting off:** everything you've planned becomes visible to your athletes straight away. Your chosen period is kept for next time.
- **Set an athlete inactive:** your setting still applies. See [Managing Your Athlete Roster](/support-center/managing-your-athlete-roster/).
- **You or the athlete end the coaching relationship:** the workouts and notes you created become visible to them.
