## My Athletes

Go to **More › My Athletes** to see everyone you coach.

On the desktop web app, My Athletes shows a summary row for each active athlete:

- **Latest workout** — their most recent completed or missed workout
- **Next event** — their next race or event
- **Planned workouts** — how many planned workouts they have from today onwards

This makes it quick to spot who has nothing planned, who missed a session, and who's racing soon.

Tap an athlete to open their profile.

## Pause coaching: set an athlete inactive

Use this when an athlete is taking a break, injured, or between blocks.

1. Open the athlete from **My Athletes**.
2. Under **Coaching status**, choose **Set inactive** and confirm.

An inactive athlete stays on your roster but is hidden from your schedule and athlete pickers. They're listed under **Inactive** on My Athletes. The athlete's own experience doesn't change.

To resume, open the athlete and choose **Set active**.

## Remove an athlete

1. Open the athlete from **My Athletes**.
2. Choose **Remove athlete from roster** and confirm.

This ends your coaching access. The athlete's account and training history stay with them. To coach them again you'll need to send a new invitation.

## Private notes

Each athlete's profile has **Private Notes**: coaching notes visible only to you. Use them for things like start date, pricing, payment status, membership type and goals. Athletes never see them.

## Get notified when athletes train

Go to **More › Settings** and, under **Coach › Notifications**, turn **Workout completed** on or off to control notifications when your athletes complete workouts.
