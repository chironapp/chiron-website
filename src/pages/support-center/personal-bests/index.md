## Where to find your personal bests

Open the **Insights** tab. The **Personal Bests** widget shows your PB and best performances over time for a chosen distance.

## Official results and GPS efforts

Chiron uses two kinds of performance:

- **Official** — a result you've recorded against a race event. Chiron uses the net time, or the gun time if there's no net time.
- **GPS** — your fastest effort at a distance within a synced workout, for example the fastest 5km inside a long run.

Your **personal best** at a distance is your fastest official result. A GPS effort is only shown as your PB when you have no official result at that distance. That way a fast segment of a training run doesn't replace the PB you raced for.

If a workout is linked to a race event with an official result at the same distance, the GPS effort from that workout is hidden so the same race isn't counted twice.

## Using the widget

- **Distance** — choose 1km, 1 Mile, 3km, 5km, 10km, Half or Marathon. It starts on the distance of your next race, or 5km if you don't have one.
- **Window** — **90d**, **12m** or **All time**. The chart shows your best effort per week, month or quarter.
- The step line on the chart shows your PB as it stood at each point in time.
- Select a bar to see that effort, including whether it was **Official** or **GPS**, and open the event or workout it came from.

## Manage your official PBs

Tap **Manage** on the widget to open **Official Personal Bests**. It lists each distance with your PB time, date and race.

- Tap a row to open the race.
- For a distance with no official result, tap **Add result**.
- To bring in many results at once, use **Import results**. See [Importing Race Results in Bulk](/support-center/how-to-import-race-results-bulk-paste-spreadsheet/).

## Turning a GPS effort into an official result

If a GPS effort was actually a race, select it in the widget and choose to add a race result. Chiron creates a completed race event linked to that workout.

## Coaches

Coaches can view an athlete's Personal Bests and Official PBs, but can't add or change results.
