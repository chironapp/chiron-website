A workout in Chiron can be **planned**, **completed**, or **missed**. The schedule shows what you planned against what you actually did.

## Add a workout to your schedule

1. Open the **Schedule** tab.
2. Tap **+**, then **Workout**. You can also tap a day on the calendar and choose **Workout** from the **Add to this day** menu.
3. Choose the activity type, such as run, ride or strength.
4. Give the workout a **Title**, for example *6 x 1km @ threshold*.
5. Set the **Date**.
6. Add a **Description** with the detail of the session: warm-up, reps, recoveries, target paces.
7. Under **Planned**, enter the planned duration and, for distance-based sports, the planned distance.
8. Tap **Save**.

The workout appears on your schedule as a planned workout.

## Planning a workout you'll repeat

To schedule the same workout on several days or weeks, set **Recurrence** above the date field before you save. See [Repeating a Workout](/support-center/repeating-a-workout/).

## Planning from your Workout Library

If you run the same sessions regularly, save them to your Workout Library and schedule them in a couple of taps. See [Scheduling Workouts from the Workout Library](/support-center/scheduling-from-workout-library/).

To save an existing workout to your library, open it, tap the **⋮** menu and choose **Add to Workout Library**.

## Strength workouts

Strength and physio workouts have their own exercise list with sets, reps and load. See [Strength & Conditioning in Chiron](/support-center/strength-and-conditioning/).

## Editing or deleting a planned workout

Open the workout, tap the **⋮** menu and choose **Edit** to change any detail. To remove it, choose **Delete** from the same menu and confirm.

## What happens when you do the workout

When you complete the session and it syncs from Strava, Chiron matches it to the planned workout on the same day. See [Completing a Workout](/support-center/completing-a-workout/).
