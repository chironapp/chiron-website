## Switch to an athlete's schedule

Chiron shows one schedule at a time: yours, or one of your athletes'.

1. Open the profile menu (your avatar). On iPhone it opens as a sheet; on the web it opens as a dialog.
2. Choose an athlete. **My Training** switches back to your own schedule.

Athletes with an upcoming event show a trophy icon in the list. Only active athletes are listed.

While you're viewing an athlete, the **Schedule** and **Insights** tabs show that athlete's training.

## Plan workouts for an athlete

With the athlete selected, add workouts, events and notes exactly as you would for yourself. Tap **+** on the Schedule tab and choose **Workout**, **Event** or **Note**. See [Planning a Workout](/support-center/planning-a-workout/) and [Repeating a Workout](/support-center/repeating-a-workout/).

## Schedule from your Workout Library

Your Workout Library belongs to you, not your athletes, so you can schedule the same sessions to any athlete.

- **iPhone:** tap **+**, choose **Workout Library**, pick a workout and schedule it once or repeating. See [Scheduling Workouts from the Workout Library](/support-center/scheduling-from-workout-library/).
- **Desktop web:** on a wide screen the Schedule shows your Workout Library beside the calendar. Drag a workout onto a day to schedule it as a planned workout straight away.

## Review completed training

Open any of the athlete's workouts to see what they completed against what was planned. Comment on the workout to give feedback. See [Comments on Workouts and Notes](/support-center/workout-and-note-comments/).

Some actions stay with the athlete: coaches can't merge an athlete's workouts, start or finish their strength workouts, or change the sets they've logged. Their Personal Bests are read-only to you.
