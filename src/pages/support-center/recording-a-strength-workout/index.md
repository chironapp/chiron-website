## Start the workout

Open your Schedule and tap today's strength workout. Tap **Start Workout** to begin.

A timer starts running from the moment you begin, recording your total session time.

## Record your exercises

For each exercise, you have two options:

- **Check off** — tap the checkbox to mark the exercise complete without logging set detail. Good for activation drills or bodyweight circuits.

<figure>
  <img
    src="/images/support-center/strength-and-conditioning/chiron-strength-workout-log-exercises.png"
    alt="Set-level logging in Chiron showing reps and load fields with a check-off option per set"
  />
  <figcaption>Set-level logging — enter actual reps and load, or check off the set</figcaption>
</figure>

- **Log by set** — tap into each set to record actual reps and load completed. Useful when your actual session differed from the plan — heavier top set, dropped reps on the last set, and so on.

<figure>
  <img
    src="/images/support-center/strength-and-conditioning/chiron-strength-edit-structured-per-set-exercises.png"
    alt="Editing a structured set in Chiron showing load type options: weight, bodyweight, duration, and custom text"
  />
  <figcaption>Adjust sets, reps, and load — choose from weight, bodyweight, duration, or custom text</figcaption>
</figure>

## Finish the workout

When done, tap **Finish Workout**.

Your completed session is saved with total duration, exercises completed, and any set-level data you recorded. It appears in your training history alongside your run and other activity data.
