There are three places you can repeat a workout:

- **Repeat** on an existing workout, to copy it to new dates.
- **Recurrence** while creating a new workout.
- Scheduling a workout from your **Workout Library**.

All three use the same scheduler.

## Repeat an existing workout

1. Open the workout on your schedule.
2. Tap the menu and choose **Repeat**.
3. Adjust any details on the **Repeat Workout** screen.
4. Set the recurrence (see below) and save.

## Once or repeating

The scheduler has two modes:

- **Once** — creates one workout on the date you choose. This is the default.
- **Repeating** — creates a workout on every selected day of the week within a date range.

When repeating, choose:

1. **Days of week** — one or more days. Selecting Tuesday and Thursday schedules the workout on both days every week.
2. **Start date** — the first day the recurrence can start. Defaults to the next occurrence of the day you were looking at.
3. **Repeat for** — **2 weeks**, **4 weeks** (the default) or **8 weeks**, or set an **End date** for a custom range.

Chiron shows a summary of how many workouts will be created before you confirm, so you can check it before saving.

## Things to know

- You can't schedule a repeating workout to start in the past.
- Each repeat is a separate workout. Editing or deleting one doesn't change the others.
- A repeating workout is always created as planned, not completed.
