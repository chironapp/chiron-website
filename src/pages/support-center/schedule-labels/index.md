A label is a coloured bar across one or more days on your schedule. Use it for anything that lasts longer than a day: a holiday or work trip, an injury or illness, a training camp, a taper, or a race week. Everyone looking at the schedule sees the period at a glance, without a note repeated on every day.

Notes are still the place for a thought about a single day. Labels are for a period of time. See [Creating Notes](/support-center/creating-notes/).

## Create a label

1. Open the **Schedule** tab.
2. Tap **+**, then **Label**. You can also tap **+** on a day and choose **Label**, which sets that day as the start date.
3. Give the label a **Title**, such as "Singapore" or "Calf strain".
4. Optionally add a **Description**, such as flight days or what your physio has cleared you to do.
5. Set the **Start** and **End** dates. For a single day, use the same date for both.
6. Choose a **Colour**.
7. Tap **Create**.

The label appears on every day it covers straight away.

## How labels look on your schedule

On iPhone, each day a label covers shows the label's bar and title above that day's workouts, including days with nothing else planned.

## Edit a label

Tap the label's bar to open it. Change the title, description, dates or colour and tap **Save**.

## Delete a label

Tap the label's bar, then tap **Delete**. The label is removed from every day it covered. Changed your mind? Tap **Undo** on the message that appears.

## Labels and your coach

Your coach sees your labels on your schedule, and can create, edit and delete them for you. If you're heading away or picking up an injury, adding a label is a quick way to let your coach know so they can plan around it.

Coaches: switch to an athlete's schedule and add a label exactly as you would on your own. See [Viewing and Planning an Athlete's Training](/support-center/planning-athlete-training/).

Labels are visible only to you and your coach. They don't appear in schedule search.
